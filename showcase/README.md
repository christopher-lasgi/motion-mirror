# Showcase: the Orbit launch film

A 30 second launch film made with motion-mirror: picture and score generated by code, no third-party footage or music. [Watch it with sound](../assets/demo-16x9.mp4) (16:9, 1280x720, 30 s).

This page is the storyboard of the final film. It is also the contract the skill works from: every row below is a mark in one events table that drives the picture and the score, so a sound change never re-renders a frame.

![Storyboard sheet: 20 frames of the film with their timecodes](../assets/storyboard-sheet.png)

## Marks

The whole film is cut from these marks, in seconds (60 frames per second):

`hook` 0.00 · `hookMemory` 1.90 · `hookBrain` 2.83 · `collapse` 3.45 · `chat` 3.80 · `reveal` 7.50 · `define` 8.80 · `dive` 11.05 · `pipeline` 11.25 · `price` 15.00 · `anyModel` 18.00 · `marquee` 22.30 · `owned` 25.32 · `outro` 26.35 · `end` 30.00

## Formats

The 16:9 master has a 9:16 cut made from the same marks and the same score. It is re-blocked through animated windows, not cropped: one window for a single subject, stacked windows for wide shots, a blurred cover behind so no window floats on black, and the chat redrawn in portrait proportions.

![The 9:16 cut, eight frames across the film](../assets/vertical-strip.png)

Scene by scene, the process followed the [pipeline](../skills/motion-mirror/references/pipeline.md) of the skill: shot map, events table, one scene at a time, proof bundle, lock, assembly, declines.
